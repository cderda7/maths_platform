## Accessors for ticket 319 (the per-group grid)

All pure, all in `lib/standings.ts` unless named:

- **The union after individual review, every group:** `groupsAt(c, session, now)[i].union` (and `simulatedBoardOf(colour, members, session, afterIndividual).problems`).
- **The timeline per question:** `timelinesAt(c, session, now)` → per group `{ colour, live, questions: QuestionTimeline[] }`; each question's visits carry `pen`, `returning`, `checks: { at, correct, lines }[]`, `leftAt`, `closedAt`, plus `status` (`ahead | working | left | solved | unsolved`) and `solvedOnReturn`. `runTimeline(run)` in `lib/groupSim.ts` reads one run.
- **The pen at a time:** `pensAt(c, session, now)` → `{ colour, pen, problem }[]` (also `standingsAt(…)[i].pen`).
- **Groups with an empty union left out:** `groupsAt`, `standingsAt`, `leaderboardAt`, `timelinesAt` and `pensAt` never list them; `sittingOut(c, session)` names them.
