# 175: Category history on the class view

**What to build:** On the class view a third button beside a student's name, **see history in…**, puts the roster into history mode for that student: every other row, the column heads and the footer fade to 30 %, and the student's six category pills widen to carry their category's name in white (at least double the pill's width, the height unchanged). Clicking a widened pill stacks that category's last five recorded statuses above it, oldest at the top, each a 13 px pill dated in white (Aug 31 · Sep 2 · Sep 3 · Sep 7 · Sep 9), a hair (2 px) apart, the newest a hair above today's; several categories can stand open at once. As soon as one is open a cream-deep rectangle covers the rows above from the Algebra column through Set, so the stacks never mix with the other students' pills; its top edge lands on the midline of the nearest pill above the stacks (a cut through a pill, visibly hiding things), or, for the top two rows, the heads are covered whole and the cream rises out of the card to the midline of the "due" line. Nothing on the roster moves or grows. The button reads **close history** while the mode is on; a click on any other student's row (or on the cream) leaves the mode and opens nothing. A drill under the same student (see dot skills) and history coexist in either order; a column view closes on entry. Confidence and Set history are deferred.

## Problem Statement

The user (2026-09-12), with a screenshot of the row buttons: "i want to add a short color history trail to the class view … performance on the last 5 assignments/assessments of that skill … add to this view a cat for 'see history in…' & then have everything fade besides the student current row. i want for each pill to get larger (like double, just in width, not in height) & to have white text inside with the category header. then, with the other student rows still faded, open up the 5 most recent evidence points … ABOVE the current pill — again with the date label. this will simply open up OVER the other students that it comes to block … have them be closer together (like a hair of space in between them) … teacher can open multiple … once a single column opens up, block it for the whole width … from algebra to set … RULE: have the white space intersect an above student halfway through a pill … have it be that cream color … teacher exits by clicking 'close history' … clicking into a different student's row will collapse all of the history pills". Settled in the interview: three buttons in today's row height (padding trimmed); a drill and history coexist on the same student; everything in the card fades; oldest at the top; the cream from Algebra to Set, cream-deep; another row's click leaves the mode and is swallowed; a widened pill again closes just its own history; an average of colours, not a trend ("a dark green a week ago means that skill was easier, not that they underperformed"); one student dark green everywhere; the top rows' cream covers the heads and spills over the "due" line without a scroll.

## Solution

- `lib/history.ts` (new): `historyFor(student, category, today)`, five `HistoryPoint`s (date, status) oldest first, seeded by student and category (FNV-1a → mulberry32) from a fixed mix around today's status: red today → red and orange; orange → mostly orange, some red, some light green; light green → orange, light green and dark green; dark green → mostly dark green, some light green; a hollow pill today → the orange mix. `ALL_SECURE_STUDENT` (Priya, dark green in every category today) is dark green on every set behind. `HISTORY_DATES` the same five for everyone. Tests in `lib/history.test.ts`.
- `app/teacher/TeacherLive.tsx`: `history` state (`{ student, open: CategoryId[] }`), `openHistory` (closes a column view and another student's drill, keeps this student's), `toggleHistory`, `leaveHistory` (a click on another row, another row's pill or the cream: leaves and swallows). The row's stack is three buttons in `ROW_BUTTON` (leading-none, 1.5 px vertical padding, 2 px gaps: 46 px, the two-button stack's height), kept in view for the student in history mode, never shown on a faded row. In history mode a pill renders `HistoryPill` (the name in 9 px semibold uppercase white, min 56 px, `interpolate-size: allow-keywords` so the width animates in browsers that can; hollow → muted text) and, when open, its five points stacked `absolute` above it at z-30, sized by the pill's own width. `HistoryBlocker`, mounted beside the card in a `relative` box while any history is open, measures the table by ResizeObserver (a drill opening, a resize) and draws the wide cream (first category head to the Set head, from the student's row top up to the nearest pill midline at or above the stacks' top, else the "due" line's midline) and an apron (the category columns only, from the row top to 2 px above the pills), both z-25 over the sticky heads, both leaving the mode on click. Faded rows are `opacity-30` tbodies; the heads' contents fade with `[&>th>*]:opacity-30` and `pointer-events-none`; the footer fades. The `UNIT 1` label beside a flat category's pill is not drawn in history mode (the widened pill names itself and would cover it).
