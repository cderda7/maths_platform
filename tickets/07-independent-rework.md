# 07: Independent rework stage

**What to build:** Before any group step the student reworks their submission alone. In this stage they see only the detective-work clue and their original transcription with no red or blue highlights. They rework on the drawpad, producing a new version; the original is preserved untouched so later screens (history, teacher before/after) can show both. Finishing rework moves them to the group stage.
